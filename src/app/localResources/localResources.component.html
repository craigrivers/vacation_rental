📍 Location: Monticello, NY (20 min away)

Why Visit? One of the largest casinos in New York, perfect for nightlife and gaming excitement.

3. Kartrite Resort & Indoor Waterpark 🌊🏄‍♂️

📍 Location: Monticello, NY (20 min away)

Activities: Water slides, lazy river, surf simulator, indoor wave pool, and arcade.

Why Visit? New York’s largest indoor waterpark—great for year-round family fun!

4. Bethel Woods Center for the Arts & Woodstock Museum 🎶🎨

📍 Location: Bethel, NY (10 min away)

Activities: Concerts, outdoor festivals, the Woodstock Museum, and cultural exhibits.

Why Visit? The legendary site of the 1969 Woodstock Festival—perfect for music and history lovers.
