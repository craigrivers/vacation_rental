Pricing and Terms

Reservations

A $500 security deposit is required to make a reservation. It is in addition to the rental fee and is returned two weeks after the rental, contingent on the house being left in the condition that it was found. Any damages will be subtracted from the security deposit prior to its return.

The rental rates are listed below. There is a 5% lodging tax that is added to each rental. The rental payment is due two weeks prior to the rental.

Payments can be made via PayPal (MonticelloCabin@gmail.com) or Zelle (MonticelloCabin@gmail.com).

To make a reservation first check that the dates you want are available Availability and then send an email to MonticelloCabin@gmail.com specifying the dates for the reservation.

Cancellation Policy

If a reservation is cancelled with 30 or more days notice, the security deposit will be fully refunded. Otherwise, the security deposit will be forfeited.
