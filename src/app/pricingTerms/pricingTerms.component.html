Pricing and Terms

Reservations

A $500 security deposit is required in addition to the rental fee. The deposit will be returned within two weeks after your stay, less any damages.

The rental rates are listed below for groups of 13 people or less, larger groups pay a little more.There is a 5% lodging tax that is added to each rental. The rental payment is due two weeks prior to the rental.

Payments can be made via PayPal (MonticelloCabin@gmail.com) or Zelle (MonticelloCabin@gmail.com).

To reserve, check availability then email MonticelloCabin@gmail.com with your desired dates.

2025 Rates

  • Full Week: $3000

Non-Summer Months

Check-In and Check-Out

Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 1:00 PM

Cancellation Policy

If a reservation is cancelled with 30 or more days notice, the security deposit will be fully refunded. Otherwise, the security deposit will be forfeited.
